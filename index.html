Membership Plans

Who It's For

Members

Log progress, check your BMI, see days left on your membership and pay by bKash or Nagad.

Trainers

Create workout plans, check members in and out and plan weekly classes.

Nutritionists

Write diet plans, calculate calories and build meal plans with local food.

Admin

Manage plans, approve staff, verify payments and watch gym statistics.

How It Works

1

Sign up

Open Join Now, choose Member, Trainer or Nutritionist and fill in the details that type needs. Trainers and nutritionists are approved by the admin.

2

Log in and pay

Use the single login page. Members pay for a plan with bKash or Nagad; the admin checks the transaction ID and starts the membership.

3

Train with a plan

Your trainer gives you workouts and checks you in, your nutritionist gives you a diet and meals, and you track your weight, BMI and goal on your dashboard.
